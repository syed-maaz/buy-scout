What do INSPECT / ASK / WATCH / REJECT mean?
INSPECTWorth a viewing. Passes all hard gates, family fields are known, and the relative fit score is ≥ 65. This is a shortlist label, not an approval or guarantee.
ASKAsk the seller first. Passes hard gates, but at least one required field is unknown: seats, doors, ISOFIX or body type, or a fuel, gearbox, damage or seller-type constraint. Not a rejection; get the missing facts before booking.
WATCHKeep watching. Passes hard gates and family fields are known, but the relative fit score is below 65. Wait for a price drop or a better car.
REJECTFailed a hard rule. Price, distance, seats, doors, ISOFIX, year, mileage, damage, commercial-only seller or a red flag. Score is 0; revisit only if the data or criteria change.
Score is a 0–100 relative fit ranking, not a probability and not a quality rating. Confidence is data completeness (0–1), not the model's certainty. A badge never verifies the seller's claims. Always check VIN, HU/AU report, service history, accident history, mileage plausibility, ownership, cold start, test drive and an independent inspection.
